Methodology

Data-Driven
📦
Data Sources
883 T20 matches across IPL 2022–2026, international T20Is (2022–2026), and bilateral T20 series. 200,999 ball-by-ball records with stroke, delivery style, dismissal type, bowling speed, and over-by-over context. Combined with playing XI data for batting order confirmation.
⚡
Pressure Definitions
Death Overs: Balls 91-120 (Overs 16-20)
Powerplay: Overs 1-6
Chase: 2nd innings batting
Knockout: Finals, Semis, Qualifiers, Eliminators

Minimum qualification: 30 balls in death overs for batters, 18 balls for bowlers.
🧮
BPI Formula
Batting Pressure Index combines four normalised components:

35% Death SR · 25% Death Average
20% Boundary % · 20% SR Uplift

A Knockout boost (15% weight) is applied for players with knockout match data, rewarding big-game temperament.
🎯
BoPI Formula
Bowling Pressure Index penalises high economy and rewards wicket-taking:

30% Death Economy (inverted) · 30% Wickets
20% Strike Rate (inverted) · 20% Eco Improvement

Knockout bowling economy adds a 15% boost for finisher-game experience.
🔄
SR Uplift Factor
A player's Death SR minus their Overall SR. This measures whether a player specifically elevates their game under pressure, rather than simply being a consistent performer. Players with high uplift are clutch specialists — they save their best for when it counts.
🏏
RR Squad Gap Analysis
All top-ranked players were filtered against the RR 2026 squad roster (27 players). Remaining targets are ranked by their composite pressure index. Auction targets prioritise players who address death batting depth, powerplay wicket-taking, and chase finishing versatility.
BPI Formula
BPI = 0.35 × norm(Death_SR) + 0.25 × norm(Death_Avg)
+ 0.20 × norm(Boundary%) + 0.20 × norm(SR_Uplift)
BPI_final = 0.85 × BPI + 0.15 × norm(Knockout_SR / 250)
BoPI Formula
BoPI = 0.30 × inv_norm(Death_Eco) + 0.30 × norm(Wickets)
+ 0.20 × inv_norm(Death_SR) + 0.20 × norm(Eco_Improvement)
BoPI_final = 0.85 × BoPI + 0.15 × inv_norm(Knockout_Eco / 15)
