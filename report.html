Composite Indices BPI & BoPI
Key Innovation — SR Uplift Factor: Instead of simply ranking players by their death-over SR, we compute SR Uplift = Death SR − Overall SR. A player with an uplift of +50 genuinely accelerates under pressure — distinguishing a clutch specialist from a player who is simply always aggressive. This separates pressure performers from consistent performers.
🏏
Batting Pressure Index (BPI)
Four components, each min-max normalised to 0–1:

35% — Death Over Strike Rate
25% — Death Over Average
20% — Boundary % in death overs
20% — SR Uplift (Death SR – Overall SR)

A Knockout boost (15% weight) applied for players with KO match data. Minimum: 30 balls in death overs.
🎯
Bowling Pressure Index (BoPI)
Four components, each min-max normalised to 0–1:

30% — Death Economy Rate (inverted — lower = better)
30% — Death Wickets taken
20% — Death Strike Rate (inverted)
20% — Eco Improvement (Overall Eco – Death Eco)

A Knockout Economy boost (15% weight) applied. Minimum: 18 balls in death overs.
BPI Formula
BPI = 0.35 × norm(Death_SR) + 0.25 × norm(Death_Avg) + 0.20 × norm(Boundary%) + 0.20 × norm(SR_Uplift)
BPI_final = 0.85 × BPI + 0.15 × norm(Knockout_SR / 250)
BoPI Formula
BoPI = 0.30 × inv_norm(Death_Eco) + 0.30 × norm(Wickets) + 0.20 × inv_norm(Death_SR) + 0.20 × norm(Eco_Improvement)
BoPI_final = 0.85 × BoPI + 0.15 × inv_norm(Knockout_Economy / 15)
